What we treat

Care for every body.
At every stage.

Whether it's a stiff back, a painful knee, recovery after surgery or a sports injury, we build a treatment plan around you, your body and your daily life.

01

Post-Operative Rehab

Guided recovery after knee or hip replacement (TKR, THR) and other surgeries, to bring back movement, strength and confidence.

02

Bone & Joint Care

Diagnosis-led treatment for stiff, weak or painful joints, so everyday movement feels easy again.

03

Chronic Pain Management

Lasting relief for arthritis, frozen shoulder, back and neck pain, through hands-on therapy and guided exercise.

Dry Needling

Targeted relief for tight, painful muscles. Reduces tension, eases pain and helps you move better.

ACL & Meniscus Rehab

Surgical and non-surgical knee rehab, from your first steps after injury to walking, climbing stairs and full activity.

PCL Injury Rehab

Stability, strength and control for the knee, step by step, until everyday movement feels natural again.

Sports & Gym Injury Rehab

Sprains, strains, tendon problems and workout injuries. We treat the injury, improve how you move, and get you safely back to your activity.
