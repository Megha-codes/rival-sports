What we do

Built for athletes.
Made for everyone.

Whether you tore a ligament on the field, hurt your shoulder under the bar, or just had surgery, we build a plan around you and your goals.

Sports & Gym Injury Rehab

Sprains, strains, tendon problems and lifting injuries. We treat the injury, fix how you move, and get you back to competing.

ACL & Meniscus Rehab

Surgical and non-surgical rehab, from the first steps after injury to running, cutting and full return to sport.

PCL Injury Rehab

Stability, strength and control for the knee, step by step, until you're ready to perform again.

Dry Needling

Targeted relief for tight, painful muscles. Reduces tension, eases pain and helps you move better.

05

Post-Operative Rehab

Guided recovery after knee or hip replacement (TKR, THR) and other surgeries, to bring back movement, strength and confidence.

06

Bone & Joint Care

Diagnosis-led treatment for stiff, weak or painful joints, so everyday movement feels easy again.

07

Chronic Pain Management

Long-term relief for arthritis, frozen shoulder, back and neck pain, built on hands-on therapy and targeted exercise.
